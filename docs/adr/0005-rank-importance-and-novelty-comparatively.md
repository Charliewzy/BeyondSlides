# Rank importance and novelty comparatively after passage preparation

Lecture passages are prepared without per-window importance or novelty ratings; after the complete chronological partition exists, BeyondSlides independently runs repeated best--worst comparisons for importance and slide-grounded novelty across shuffled small groups. It aggregates each passage's most and least selections into a normalized best--worst balance and lecture-relative percentile. Reports expose that percentile directly through top-percentage controls and retain tied passages together; the derived `1..5` display level remains compatibility and inspection data. This prevents score prompts from influencing passage boundaries and avoids treating an LLM's poorly calibrated absolute ratings as measurements; neither percentiles nor levels are comparable across lectures.
