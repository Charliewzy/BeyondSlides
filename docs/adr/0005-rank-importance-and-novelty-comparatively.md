# Rank importance and novelty comparatively after passage preparation

Lecture passages are prepared without per-window importance or novelty ratings; after the complete chronological partition exists, BeyondSlides independently runs repeated best--worst comparisons for importance and slide-grounded novelty across shuffled small groups. It aggregates each passage's most and least selections into a normalized best--worst balance and lecture-relative percentile, then derives the existing `1..5` display level from percentile bands. This prevents score prompts from influencing passage boundaries and avoids treating an LLM's poorly calibrated absolute ratings as measurements; the stored comparison evidence remains inspectable, while levels are only presentation data and are not comparable across lectures.
